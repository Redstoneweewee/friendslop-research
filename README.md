# Friendslop Games Market Research

What kind of friendslop game should we make for a summer 2027 Steam release, and how crowded is the market
getting? The page compares first-year hit rates (500+ reviews in the launch month plus eleven months) by
activity and price, against other online games and all of Steam, and estimates friendslop releases through
Q3 2027.

Read it at https://redstoneweewee.github.io/friendslop-research/

- `index.html`: the page (charts and tables build from data embedded in it)
- `data/`: CSVs behind every table and chart, plus one row per online launch so the numbers can be checked in Excel
- `friendslop_games.csv`: every game classified as friendslop

Steam data was collected on 19 October 2026 from the Steam store, query, review and review-history APIs.
